# MJ favicon variants (D-20)

Glyph outlines from Funnel Display, converted to paths (no text element). No rounded tile, no gradient. Each SVG switches colors with prefers-color-scheme.

- mj-a.svg: terracotta MJ (Bold) on a full-bleed paper square, dark variant on #0d1014. Reads clearly at 16 px, one accent only, the most "brand mark" of the three.
- mj-b.svg: ink MJ (SemiBold) with the J in terracotta, full-bleed paper. At 16 px the M and J stay separate and the J carries the accent; two colors, still one accent.
- mj-c.svg: terracotta MJ (Bold) on a transparent background. It takes the tab color, so it looks lightest in the tab, but with no square edge it is the weakest at 16 px on tab colors that are not paper or ink.

Recommendation: a. Highest contrast at 16 px in light and dark tabs, single accent, and it matches the DESIGN.md palette without extra decisions.
